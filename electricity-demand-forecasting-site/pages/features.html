Feature Engineering

Features are designed to capture seasonality, autocorrelation, and nonlinear drivers (weather/calendar).

Feature Groups

  • Lag features: load(t−1), load(t−24), load(t−168), rolling means
  • Calendar: hour-of-day, weekday, weekend flag, season, holidays (optional)
  • Weather: temperature, wind speed, solar radiation, cloud cover (+ lags)

Why this helps

  • SARIMA captures structured seasonality from the target series
  • LSTM and hybrid residual models benefit from exogenous drivers and long/short memory
