Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

EDA guides model selection and feature design, with a special focus on peak-load regimes.

Temporal Trends

  • Daily and weekly patterns in electricity demand
  • Monthly/seasonal effects (winter vs summer)
  • Long-term changes and anomalies

Seasonality

  • Decomposition to understand trend + seasonality + residual
  • Weekly and annual seasonality strength (important for SARIMA orders)

Correlation & Weather Effects

  • Relationship between load and temperature
  • Effects of wind/solar and weather-driven changes

Peak-Load Focus (Core)

  • Define peak threshold: e.g., 90th percentile of load in test period
  • Compare peak vs normal hours across time-of-day and seasons
  • Prepare error diagnostics: error vs load level, peak-only error distribution
