What is included

Ensemble forecasting is implemented by aligning SARIMA, LSTM, and Hybrid predictions by timestamp and comparing multiple combination strategies. It evaluates simple averaging baselines (mean/median) and then applies stacking with linear models: Ridge-based stacking (with and without intercept) and a non-negative least squares (NNLS) setup to derive interpretable convex weights. Performance is measured both overall and specifically on peak demand periods defined by a 90th-percentile (p90) threshold. In addition, a residual-correction ensemble learns the LSTM residual using SARIMA and Hybrid predictions as inputs, producing an adjusted forecast (LSTM + predicted residual). Finally, a regime-switching ensemble chooses between the plain LSTM and the residual-corrected LSTM depending on whether demand is above the p90 threshold, targeting better peak accuracy without sacrificing overall performance.
