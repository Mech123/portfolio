Related Work

Short-term load forecasting (STLF) is a key task for transmission system and grid operators. Research broadly splits into statistical forecasting, deep learning approaches, hybrid/ensemble strategies, and explainability for operational trust.

Statistical forecasting (SARIMA)

  • Interpretable seasonality/trend modeling
  • Low inference cost and strong baselines
  • May struggle with sudden regime changes and spikes

Deep learning (LSTM)

  • Learns nonlinear relationships between load, weather, and calendar features
  • Often improves accuracy in complex conditions
  • Needs explainability and careful validation

Hybrid and ensemble methods

  • Hybrid: SARIMA captures structured seasonality; LSTM models residual nonlinear components
  • Ensemble: combines forecasts to reduce variance and increase robustness during peaks

Explainability (XAI)

  • SHAP/LIME explain individual predictions and feature contributions
  • Useful for comparing peak vs normal hours and debugging mispredictions
