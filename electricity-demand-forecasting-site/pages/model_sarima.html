Summary

SARIMA shows strong seasonality fitting but poor generalization when the test distribution shifts and during sharp peak events. On the forecasts CSV, the model has a large positive bias (actual − forecast), meaning it tends to under-predict demand, and peak-region fit degrades significantly.
