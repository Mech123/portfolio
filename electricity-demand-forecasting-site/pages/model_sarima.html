SARIMA Model

Back to Models

SARIMA is a classical seasonal time-series model. It captures daily/weekly patterns well, and provides a strong interpretable baseline for short-term load forecasting.

  • Forecasts (CSV): assets/data/sarima/sarima_forecasts.csv
  • Notebook (GitHub): Open sarima_model.ipynb
