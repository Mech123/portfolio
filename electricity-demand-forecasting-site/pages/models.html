Folder structure (recommended)

Keep each model fully isolated to avoid filename conflicts. 

assets/
  data/
    sarima/    (sarima_metrics.json, sarima_forecasts.csv, ...)
    lstm/      (metrics.json, lstm_predictions.csv, best_model.h5, ...)
    hybrid/    (metrics_hybrid.json, hybrid_predictions.csv, best_residual_model.h5, ...)
    ensemble/  (metrics_ensemble.json, ensemble_predictions.csv, ...)
  images/
    models/
      sarima/   (00_data_shift_analysis.png, ...)
      lstm/     (training_history.png, predictions_visualization.png)
      hybrid/   (training_history_hybrid.png, ...)
      ensemble/ (your ensemble plots)
