Models & Results

We compare four pipelines for short-term demand forecasting (30 min to 1 hour ahead) and evaluate both overall accuracy and peak-hour stability.

1) SARIMA (Statistical baseline)

  • Captures seasonality and trend in the demand series
  • Rolling-origin backtesting and residual diagnostics (ACF/PACF, Ljung–Box)
  • Model selection via AIC/BIC

2) LSTM (Deep learning)

  • Learns nonlinear interactions between load history, weather and calendar features
  • Sliding-window sequences, validation-based tuning, learning curves

3) Hybrid SARIMA + LSTM (Residual learning)

  • Train SARIMA → compute residuals (actual − SARIMA)
  • Train LSTM to predict residuals using exogenous inputs
  • Final forecast = SARIMA forecast + predicted residual

4) Ensemble (SARIMA + LSTM)

  • Combine forecasts via weighted averaging (weights from validation performance)
  • Goal: reduce variance and improve robustness during peaks

Evaluation Metrics

  • Overall: MAE, RMSE, sMAPE
  • Peak-focused: MAE_peak, RMSE_peak where load ≥ 90th percentile
  • Compute: training time, inference latency
