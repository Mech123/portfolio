Models & Results

We compare four forecasting pipelines for short-term demand forecasting and evaluate overall performance and peak-load stability.

SARIMA

  • Statistical baseline capturing trend and seasonality
  • Rolling-origin backtesting and diagnostics

LSTM

  • Deep learning model for nonlinear patterns
  • Uses lagged sequences and exogenous features

Hybrid SARIMA + LSTM

  • SARIMA models structured components
  • LSTM predicts residual errors
  • Final forecast = SARIMA forecast + LSTM residual forecast

Ensemble

  • Combines SARIMA and LSTM forecasts
  • Weighted averaging improves robustness and reduces variance

Evaluation Metrics

  • MAE, RMSE, sMAPE for all hours
  • MAE and RMSE for peak hours (percentile-based threshold)
  • Training time and inference latency
