Pre Processing

Pipeline Summary

  1. Load data: import OPSD time series and merge with weather variables.
  2. Clean timestamps: enforce hourly resolution and correct ordering.
  3. Handle missing values: fill gaps and ensure a continuous time index.
  4. Calendar features: hour/day/week/month, weekend indicators, holiday flags.
  5. Weather feature preparation: align weather to timestamps and standardize where needed.
  6. Finalize dataset: export processed 60-min dataset used by all models.
