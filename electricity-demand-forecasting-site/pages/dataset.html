Dataset

We use the Open Power System Data (OPSD) time-series dataset and focus on Germany for short-horizon demand forecasting (30 minutes to 1 hour ahead).

Source

  • Dataset: OPSD – Time Series (Version 2020-10-06)
  • Coverage: ~35 European countries (we use Germany only)
  • Frequency: hourly (some series may include sub-hourly entries)
  • Format: CSV/Parquet with standardized metadata

Key Variables

  • Load: electricity demand (actual; forecasted if available)
  • Generation: solar/wind/hydro/conventional
  • Weather: temperature, wind speed, solar radiation, cloud cover (if available)
  • Calendar: timestamps, weekday/weekend, holidays (derived)

Forecasting Target

  • Predict demand at t + 30 min and t + 1 hour horizons
  • Evaluate both all hours and peak hours regimes
