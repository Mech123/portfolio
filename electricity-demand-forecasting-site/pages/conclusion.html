Conclusion

This project evaluates statistical and deep learning approaches for short-term electricity demand forecasting in Germany, with special focus on peak-load stability and explainability.

Main Outcomes

  • Compare SARIMA and LSTM performance for short horizons
  • Measure stability during peak-demand hours
  • Assess whether hybrid and ensemble strategies improve peak reliability
  • Use explainability methods to support model trust and debugging

Next Steps

  • Finalize experiments and publish results tables and plots on the website
  • Add explainability visuals for peak and non-peak cases
  • Optional: add uncertainty estimates and prediction intervals
