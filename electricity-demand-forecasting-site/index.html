Project Introduction

Comparative study of SARIMA, LSTM, Hybrid SARIMA+LSTM, and ensemble approaches for short-term electricity demand forecasting (30 minutes to 1 hour ahead), with a focus on peak-load stability and explainability using SHAP and LIME.
