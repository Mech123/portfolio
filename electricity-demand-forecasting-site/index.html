Team Introduction

Comparative study of SARIMA, LSTM, Hybrid SARIMA+LSTM, and Ensemble approaches for short-term electricity demand forecasting (30 minutes to 1 hour ahead), with a special focus on peak-load stability and model explainability (SHAP, LIME).

Quick Summary

  • Goal: accurate + stable demand forecasts for grid operations (Germany).
  • Data: OPSD time series (load + generation + weather + calendar).
  • Models: SARIMA baseline, LSTM nonlinear model, Hybrid residual learning, Ensemble averaging.
  • Peak focus: evaluate errors for all hours vs peak hours (≥ 90th percentile load).
  • XAI: SHAP/LIME explanations to improve trust and debugging.
